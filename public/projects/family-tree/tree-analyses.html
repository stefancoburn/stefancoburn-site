Three analyses of the tree

Built from 864 individuals and 143 families in the Ancestry export, with 672 distinct places geocoded.

Arcs run birthplace → place of death. Grey dots mark the 212 people who died within 25 miles of where they were born. Two red arcs end mid-ocean: relatives who died at sea during the crossing.

Each dot is one person. The black line is the median for each birth decade, drawn only where at least six people have both dates.

Counts only children recorded in the tree, so single-child families are likely undercounted — a family enters the tree through one documented child and siblings may be missing.
