Size and shape
aSemi-major axis km

Half the long diameter of the ellipse. It alone sets the period and the orbital energy.

eEccentricity

0 is a circle; toward 1 the ellipse stretches out. Earth sits at a focus, offset from the center by ae.

Orientation of the plane
iInclination °

Tilt of the orbital plane against the equator, hinged on the line of nodes. Also the angle between Z and the orbit normal h.

ΩRight ascension of the ascending node °

Swings the tilted plane around the polar axis. Measured east along the equator from ♈ to where the satellite crosses heading north.

Orientation within the plane
ωArgument of periapsis °

Spins the ellipse inside its own plane. Measured from the ascending node to periapsis, in the direction of motion.

Position on the orbit
νTrue anomaly °

Where the satellite is right now: the angle from periapsis to the satellite, seen from Earth's center. Press Play to let time move it.
